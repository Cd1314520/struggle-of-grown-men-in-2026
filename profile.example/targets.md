### Employer types

The assessment sorts every employer into one of these. The practice phase applies only where your real search would not.

- **Tech company at scale** (`tech_scaled`): software or tech-first company past early stage (roughly 300+ people, or a large established product) whose core product is the software.
- **Early-stage startup** (`startup`): small or early tech company (roughly under 300 people, or seed to Series B).
- **Non-tech enterprise** (`non_tech_enterprise`): an employer whose business is not technology (insurance, banking, energy, healthcare, retail, manufacturing, logistics, media, education).
- **IT services or consultancy** (`it_services`): IT services, consulting, staffing, outsourcing, systems integrators, agencies.
- **Government or public sector** (`government`): government, public universities, and contractors whose work is for the government.

### Never (both phases)

- Internships, co-ops, apprenticeships and other student or temporary training programmes.
- Senior, staff, principal, lead or manager roles, or 5+ years required.

## Practice phase

Goal: interview practice. Apply to roles you would decline in the real search, where your background still makes you a credible candidate, so you get real screens and interviews before you approach the roles you care about.

### What makes a role practice

A role is practice when its employer is one the real search rules out, on positive evidence:

- an early-stage startup, a non-tech enterprise, an IT services firm or consultancy, or government (see Employer types); or
- an employer on the practice list in `profile/employers.yml`.

### Hold back (do not apply now)

- Employers on the frontier list in `profile/employers.yml`.
- Every tech company at scale: a large employer keeps one record of a candidate across teams.

### Interview fit

Even when the employer makes it practice, the role must give genuine interview practice: hands-on engineering at your level, where your resume plausibly clears the screen and the process is likely to include a technical interview.

### Triage guidance

Coarse triage sees only title, company and location. Keep plausible engineering roles at any employer so the full assessment can sort them into practice or hold. Drop titles that are clearly under Never.
