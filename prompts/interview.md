## The note

In Markdown, under these headings, about 300 words in all:

- **What the role needs**: the three or four things the posting and the next step will test, in plain words.
- **Stories to tell**: the three or four stories from the story bank that fit those best, by their number and title, with one line each on which question they answer and the detail to lead with. Use the story bank's question map where it fits.
- **Facts to have ready**: the numbers and specifics from the resume and the answer bank that match the role.
- **Gaps to review**: what the posting asks for that the resume does not show, with one line each on what to read up on or how to answer honestly when it comes up. Take the concerns above into account.
- **Questions to ask them**: two or three, specific to this team and posting.

Use only facts from the resume, the answer bank and the story bank: never add experience, tools or numbers. A story the bank marks as a teammate's work, or as not finished, stays described that way. Follow the writing rules.
