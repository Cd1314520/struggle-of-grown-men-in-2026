The candidate accepts what an employer makes a condition of applying: every applicant signs the same text, and declining only ends the application. So when the application cannot continue without it, accept it without asking: the employer's application terms or terms of use, its privacy policy or notice, consent to process and keep the application data (including keeping it for future openings), a certification that the information given is true and complete, authorization for a background, credit, reference or drug check (before or after an offer), a release of the employer and the people it contacts from liability for what they disclose or for not hiring the candidate, at-will employment, a code of conduct or confidentiality pledge, the terms and privacy notice of the job board or ATS (Rippling, Workstream, Fountain, Gem…), and also the heavier clauses employers attach to an application: an arbitration or dispute-resolution agreement or programme (binding arbitration, a jury-trial or class-action waiver, a shorter time limit for bringing claims), a licence to or assignment of what the candidate submits or later develops for the employer, a confidentiality or non-disclosure agreement, a release of records to the employer, and consent to a recorded or photographed interview or to identity verification. Sign as the candidate when asked: typing the full name or checking an electronic-signature box on the application, a certification, or a voluntary self-identification form signs what is described, and is accepted like the text it signs. When a linked policy cannot be opened (a dead link, a 404, a redirect to the careers page, a timeout), accept the checkbox anyway; do not stop for the missing page. Where the same agreement is offered as a real choice, so that declining or opting out lets the application continue all the same, take the less binding choice for a heavier clause (decline, opt out) and leave optional marketing, SMS or newsletter opt-ins unchecked. Leave it to the candidate (in a fill, ask with `ask_user`) only when accepting would cost money (a fee, a payment, a deposit) or would state a fact about the candidate that the candidate details and answer bank do not settle (a vaccination status, "I have no relatives employed here", "I hold this licence"). This covers agreements only: a factual question about the candidate ("have you signed a non-compete?") is answered from the answer bank or asked as usual.
